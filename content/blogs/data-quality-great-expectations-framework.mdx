## Data Architecture

Data engineering is the discipline of building systems that collect, process, store, and serve data at scale. Data Quality Great Expectations Framework is a fundamental concept in this domain, directly impacting the reliability, performance, and cost-effectiveness of your data infrastructure. Modern data platforms combine batch processing, real-time streaming, and machine learning capabilities to support a wide range of analytical and operational use cases.

The data lifecycle — from ingestion through transformation, storage, and serving — involves a complex ecosystem of tools and practices. Data Quality Great Expectations Framework addresses one of the most critical stages in this lifecycle. Understanding the trade-offs between different approaches and tools helps data engineers design systems that meet their organization's specific requirements.

Data quality is a persistent challenge in data engineering. Data Quality Great Expectations Framework helps ensure that the data flowing through your pipelines is accurate, complete, and timely. Data validation frameworks, schema enforcement, and monitoring dashboards help detect and address quality issues before they impact downstream consumers.
